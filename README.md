# FMGE_Chatbot
My buddy is prepping for the FMGE, and let's be real, it is a massive exam. We're talking 300 questions that test clinical reasoning, not just rote memory. Standard ChatGPT tends to hallucinate or give generic Western advice when asked about specific Indian medical guidelines or complex clinical vignettes. Everything via opensource. 

So, we are building a custom AI assistant that actually knows its stuff.

What this chatbot needs to do(Primary functions):

1. Act as a 24/7 tutor: Explain complex concepts, clear up doubts, and break down exactly why a specific drug or treatment is the right choice.

2. Run mock tests: Pull from past years' questions, simulate the exam environment, track his score, and explain the rationale behind correct and incorrect options.
